## Kilauea Volcanic Eruption of 2018
Short video of the map layers used to create a comparison of events during the Kilauea Volcanic Eruption in 2018 (images/Kilauea Map 2018_OP.png)

*Interactive version, live as of October 2026: [[StroyApp](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/71f2071d61f544c4a08dc0dc31eaaf54)]*

**Question:** A geographic question that this map answers is that of the total extent of lava flow has affected the surrounding area.

**Data:** ISO 19139 Geographic Information - Metadata - Implementation Specification, spatial-admin_ucsb, 2024, ArcGIS Online

**Method:** The methodology I used to answer the geographic that was posed; was that of layering imagines from Landsat to compare different events of time. From the Landsat data, I was able to change to color specturm used to help the viewer to see the layers along with lava flows using infrared specturm to see througt the volcanic ash in the sky. Using all the layers and changes, I created a live webpage to host the maps along with giving addtional information about the map. 

**A design choice I made and why:** A design choice I had made was when making the layers of map was that of changing the brightness and contrast of the map due to the original values that were given to the imagines caused the layers to either be to dull or the exposure was to high to see anything that I  wanted to portray. 

**A limitation of this map:** A limitation that this map has is that of scale that the area of lava takes up. There is no scale bar that is provided so it is up to the viewer to interpret the area that was damaged from the lava flows.

## Covid-19 rates and cases numbers in New York City
! [A short video of the map to show comparison of Covid-19 rates to case counts in Ney York City](images/DiseaseMapping_OP_vid (2).mp4)

*Interactive version, live as of October 2026: [[SwipeApp](https://rccgis24.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/basic/index.html?appid=044447231e064d7eab122766ab42567e)]*

**Question:** A geographic question that this map answers is that how did Covid-19 affect New York's populated areas compared to areas where it is not as heavily populated. 

**Data:** COVID19_ZCTA, GGA_Admin, 2024, ArcGIS Online

**Method:** A Methodology that I used to help answer this question is by using colored zones to show either the seveirty of rates of Covid-19 in the area or the areas population.  

**A design choice I made and why:** I chose the color gratiated to help contrast the difference between rates and cases as this shows that there was a inverse relationship between the two. Areas with higher population had lower rates compared to areas with lower popuartion having high rates of Covid-19

**A limitation of this map:** A limitation that this map has is that of not being able to show a perfect relationship between rates and cases as the data only infers on the numbers given and not actuality of the situation that has taken place.
